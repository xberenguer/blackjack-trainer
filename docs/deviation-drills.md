# Targeted deviation practice and V8 audit

The optional practice is a named BJA2018HiLoTruncatedV1 index policy over the existing rule-specific basic policy. It is not exact remaining-composition EV and does not inspect a live dealer hole, unseen burn, future card sequence or live RNG. The practice generator has an independent seeded stream; tests verify that opening, grading, helping and replaying drills leaves the live game phase/cards/chips/RNG unchanged.

The 31 targets come from independently transcribed playing indices in the [BJA H17 chart](https://www.blackjackapprenticeship.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/BJA_H17.pdf) and [S17 chart](https://www.blackjackapprenticeship.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/BJA_S17.pdf), including insurance. The [BJA conversion guide](https://www.blackjackapprenticeship.com/true-count-conversion-guide/) describes discarding the fractional part. Count conversion is explicitly signed truncation. The chart's zero exceptions use strict running-count sign; for example positive RC1 over three decks has integer TC0 while the hard16-vs10 positive-RC index is reached. Sources were checked on 2026-11-18. These are published policy boundaries, not an independent EV proof of every index.

Generated contexts are checked for rule/profile shape, legal masks/funding and possible exposed-card populations. The count and divisor are given decision inputs. The first two cards are not artificially deprived of surrender: applicable hard15/16 Hit/Stand targets on LS profiles use a real three-card post-hit hand. Every generated playing context is tested against a separately computed full action menu. Surrender and legal pair decisions retain priority; unsupported profile targets are explicitly unavailable rather than silently substituted. Mixed practice skips unsupported targets.

Question output contains no reference or threshold. Given-context pages do not reveal an answer or mark help. Explicit reference viewing before submission marks the pending attempt assisted and cannot be undone by an invalid answer or navigation. Cancellation consumes the token without scoring. Duplicate submissions retain the first answer. Model totals distinguish original unassisted correct/errors, original assisted attempts, and the equivalent replay counters. Each attempt retains complete original visible cards, rules, count/divisor, funding, legal actions, policy and replay origin. The 128-attempt history ring does not erase cumulative counters or reopen consumed tokens.

The question screen uses 55×80 approved player cards at x8/65/122,y20, a 35×50 dealer upcard at196,20, a compact 13px rule header, RC/TC at 11px on the right, and one selected-choice bar. Original-evidence history has six pages per retained attempt: result, threshold/reference, basic/legal-fallback explanation, full fixed rules, count/divisor, and funding/provenance. First/replay totals and per-target first-attempt counts have separate accuracy pages.

The original V8 public save layout is 2636 bytes; current V10 retains that size. Existing V7 fields are unchanged; V10 fills previously reserved bytes202/203 with audio and speed preferences. Bytes 580/581/582 store target/boundary mix/practice profile; byte 583 was reserved zero in V8 and stores the opt-in resume preference in V9. Two independent 1024-byte counter groups follow: first 584–1607 and replay 1608–2631, each overall plus 31 per-index groups of four little-endian u64 values. CRC32 occupies 2632–2635. V1–V7 initialize those preferences to mixed/H17 and zero drill totals while retaining all prior scores, settings, seats and wager policy. V8 stores aggregate progress and preferences; pending questions/history/RNG are deliberately not public save data. The separate V9-era private resume codec preserves pending/help/token/replay provenance and validates it without regrading. Pending questions, histories and RNG remain absent from the public packet.

Validation includes independent primary boundary fixtures for every target, 53,802 reference comparisons against the released policy, 5,000 reproducible generated questions, full-menu legality, funding/profile/count-feasibility guards, token/help/replay/rollover/counter-cap invariants, App navigation and live-game isolation, and independent binary fixtures V1–V8 including counters above 2^53. Actual WASM/platform/Chrome tests retain the existing game/wager/seating coverage and exercise the new context/help/result/stats path. Physical-device acceptance remains a separate final integration check.
