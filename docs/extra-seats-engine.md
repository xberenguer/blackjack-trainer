# Optional seats and deeper shoes

This engine was introduced in 0.6 and ships in the shared Rust app. Public seat preferences migrate through the current V10 codec; optional private resume also preserves live tablemates, their held cards and the visibility queues. The [rules audit](rules-audit.md) states every implemented condition and the limits of casino matching.

`configure_seats(extra, player)` accepts zero to six extra seats and a controlled position from zero through `extra`, only between rounds. Zero extra seats preserves the existing shuffle RNG calls, player cut, action behavior, ledgers, card positions and messages. `OtherPlayers` is appended to the phase enum; `other_step()` advances one simulated tablemate decision. The controller can display each new exposed card before continuing. Main player and tablemate bets, split/double costs and settlements remain independent.

The pool records a physical identity, stable counting identity and Unseen/Held/Discard zone for every card. Normal zero-seat counting identities remain the original shuffled shoe positions, preserving the old trace. Cards in completed bust/surrender/paid-natural hands may enter the discard rack; retained review snapshots do not make those cards held. Per-hand ownership flags prevent historical snapshots from discarding a card again after it has been recycled into another hand.

At exhaustion, only Discard cards are mixed and one is burned unseen. Held cards remain outside the recovery pool, all original bets/actions continue, the count epoch advances and a complete shuffle is forced after the round. The current-epoch exposure ledger retains only held known faces across that shuffle, then adds newly exposed draws. `observe_exposed()` feeds that ledger to `Tracker`; it never visits a hidden face. Table/App integration must gate this observation during a progressive reveal and must not re-observe old mixed review cards directly. `total_cards() - position` still equals the actual available undealt count after recovery, preserving existing count/checkpoint APIs.

V6 remains 336 bytes: schema version 6, byte 200 extra seats, byte 201 controlled position, bytes 202–203 reserved zero, all prior counter offsets unchanged, CRC at 332. V1–V5 imports default to zero extras and position zero and retain all exact 64-bit totals. Existing penetration is byte 12. V7 wager preferences can retain that prefix and append their own data before a moved CRC.

Validation covers all controlled positions, ordinary/80/85/90% seven-seat runs, independent scripted double/split-aces/surrender/peek/turn-order outcomes, exact card ownership and independent chip ledgers, deduplicated exposed-only counts, a complete physically valid ordered shoe that reaches exhaustion through actual prior rounds, targeted epoch-reset/hole-hiding recovery fixtures, and default-path reference replay. The 38,400 seeded seven-seat rounds encountered no natural exhaustion; the ordered-shoe and forced fixtures explicitly exercise it instead. This distinction is retained in the test output.

No additional-seat screen, save integration or hardware observation is silently implied by these backend tests. The App branch owns visible tablemate cards, between-round settings, profile/depth controls, busy/tick handling, and the combined browser/device acceptance.
